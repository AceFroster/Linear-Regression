# 🎉 Welcome to my very first Data Science project! 🎉
I’m thrilled to share it with you. In this project, I took a dive into the E-commerce Dataset from Kaggle (because who doesn’t love some shopping data?) and built a Linear Regression model to predict how much a customer will spend over the next year, based on their previous shopping habits.



## 🧠 What I Did (In Case You Care):

 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):
     I ran some basic EDA to figure out how the data works. Spoiler alert: There were some missing values, some patterns, and, you know, a typical dataset that didn’t tell me all its secrets right away.

 Built a Linear Regression Model:
     After getting comfortable with the data (and maybe convincing it to talk to me), I built a simple linear regression model to predict how much a customer would spend over the next year. Linear 
     regression: because who needs fancy algorithms when you can just draw a line?

## 📚 What I Learned (In Case You Don’t Trust Me Yet):

1) How to approach a real-world dataset and build a model from scratch:
   Turns out, building a model from scratch is a lot like making a sandwich. You just need the right ingredients and a little bit of patience.

2) The importance of understanding relationships in the data:
    Relationships matter. Not just in life, but also in data. Understanding how variables interact is key. Who knew?

3) How customer behavior influences purchasing patterns:
   Apparently, people like to spend money in patterns. Go figure.

## 🏆 Outcome

In the end, I got a working model that can predict how much a customer will spend over the next year. It’s not perfect, but hey, it’s a start. And I’ve learned a ton along the way.
